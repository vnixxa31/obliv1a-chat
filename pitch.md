Obliv1a Chat is the AI chat application of Obliv1a. Behind the scenes, it runs Open WebUI, Bifrost, and some custom software I wrote. It is available free to try. Free users have access to the Obliv1a Flash model. Starter users get expanded access to Flash. Pro users also get access to Obliv1a Pro. There is a future planned tier called Max that provides access to Obliv1a Max. Free is free. Starter is $10/month, Pro is $25/month, and Max is planned $50/month. Obliv1a Flash is the only model that doesn't have vision capabilities. The service is only available via waitlist for now. 


## What is it, and is it like ChatGPT?

1. "What actually is this? Is it just ChatGPT with a different name?"
Sort of. The difference is that this is hosted on my hardware, without any training being done on your chats, so they stay private, on servers I own. It also aims to be cheaper than the paid tiers of ChatGPT and provide more on the free tier than ChatGPT.
2. "Can I do the same things I do on ChatGPT — write emails, plan meals, explain things, help with homework?"
Like any chatbot, you can write emails, plan meals, have things explained to you, and get help with homework.
3. "Do I need to know anything technical to use it? Like, do I need to set up an account or install software?"
No technical expertise needed. I even have a youtube video explaining how to use the basic features. 
4. "Is this a website I open in my browser, or do I download an app?"
It's just a website you access in any browser, with a mobile app on the way.
5. "Who made this? Is it from a big company or regular people?"
The software is free and open-source, available for anyone to read and audit. The software was stitched together, along with some of my own custom software, by me.
6. "Is it free, or do I pay? If I pay, what do I get for it?"
There is a generous free tier. Payment is done through a Stripe payment portal.
7. "How is this different from just using the free version of ChatGPT or Gemini?"
ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude all retain your chats and use them to train their models. Note the recent NYT lawsuit, that revealed that OpenAI was even saving deleted chats. The AI provider we use internally has a zero data retention (ZDR) policy, meaning that they do not store any of your chats on their servers. Your chats are stored securely on my hardware.

## Privacy and data (the "not trained on your data" differentiator)

8. "If I type stuff into it, who can see it? Just me?"
In order to process your prompt and generate a response, our AI provider can, indeed, see your prompt and the response. However, the trail ends there. After computing the response, the provider retains no part of your prompt or the assistant response. In order to support saving conversations, your chats are also stored on my own servers.
9. "You said it's not trained on my data. Does that mean my conversations are never used to 'learn' or improve the AI? Is that guaranteed, or just a promise?"
That is guaranteed. You can read more at https://trust.novita.ai, our service provider. The provider is contractually obligated to never store your chats or use them to train or improve AI models.
10. "Are my chats saved anywhere? If so, where, and who has access? Can I delete them?"
When you delete a chat, it is gone, and cannot be recovered. Your chats are saved for you and you alone on my private server.
11. "Do you read my conversations, or does any company?"
The administrator has access to account controls, and can thus technically access a user's account for troubleshooting purposes, however we will *never* do this unless requested by the user.
12. "If I ask it something personal or private, is that going to come back to me later or get shared?"
As long as your question does not violate our terms of service (illegal content), then the world will be none the wiser to your chats. In the case of severe TOS violations, we may be obligated to turn data over to law enforcement.
13. "Where does the data physically live? Is it in a server in another country?"
It is on a computer in my basement. No one has access except me.
14. "Can my kids use it safely? Is there anything stopping them from seeing stuff they shouldn't?"
Anyone under the age of 18 should not have access to AI tools. The use of Obliv1a Chat by anyone under the age of 18 is a TOS violation and may result in a ban.
